exercise isolated fixtures, not live hosts. A negative claim applies only to the
asserted route/boundary and supplied synthetic inputs.

Control logs and exact candidate receipts belong in the producer/reviewer evidence
packet. Controls are bounded source overlays, never production switches. A plant
with no intended named assertion failure is a survivor and cannot support a row.

Out of contract: old or unadmitted consumers, other roots/users/routes, stopping
or relaunching agents, live consent, installation/deployment, privileged or
foreign-owned roots, cryptographic human-presence grants, receiver cancellation,
remote quiescence, exhaustive fault/power-loss modeling and hard kernel syscall
deadlines. These are excluded by the scope/admission and quiescence contract;
they are not established by passing local fixtures. macOS execution remains a
separate runtime qualification, even if cross-compilation succeeds.
